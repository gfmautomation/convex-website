Proposed Torrens lots

AI review of contracts for unregistered land in NSW

For proposed lots in a draft deposited plan, usually in a new estate. Convex reviews the contract with the draft plan, the draft 88B instrument and the estate's design requirements.

What Convex checks

What Convex looks for in unregistered land contracts

Draft deposited plan

Lot number, area and dimensions, and clauses allowing the final lot to differ from the draft within a tolerance.

Draft 88B instrument

Proposed easements, restrictions on use and positive covenants, including building envelopes, materials, fencing and retaining walls.

Design guidelines

Developer approval of house plans, minimum dwelling size, facade and landscaping requirements, and any bond or approval fee.

Building timeframes

Covenants to start or finish building within a period, and any buy-back or penalty if they are not met.

Registration and sunset

The expected registration date, the sunset date, the vendor's rights to rescind, and the period to complete after notice of registration.

Services and site

Road access, services, bushfire and flood affectations, and conditions about the state of the land at completion.

Documents

What Convex reads

Convex reviews the contract together with the documents attached to it.

  • The contract and special conditions
  • Draft deposited plan
  • Draft section 88B instrument
  • Estate design guidelines where attached
  • Title search for the parent lot
  • Section 10.7 planning certificate for the parent lot

Questions

Unregistered land contract review FAQs

Does Convex review the building contract?
No. Convex reviews the land contract only. See house and land contract review.
Does Convex read estate design guidelines?
Yes, where they are attached to the contract or referred to in the 88B instrument and included in the pack.
Can Convex tell me when the lot will register?
Convex reports the dates and notice periods in the contract. It cannot predict when the plan will actually register.
Is off-the-plan strata covered here?
No. Off-the-plan strata is covered on the off-the-plan page.
