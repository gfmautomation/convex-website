What to look for in an AI contract review tool

There are now several AI tools that read contracts of sale. They differ in who built them, what they read, and whether the output is something you would put your name to. These are the questions worth asking, with Convex's answers.

Questions to ask

8 questions to ask any AI contract review tool

QuestionWhy it mattersConvex
Is it built for NSW conveyancing, or adapted from a general tool?Contract structure, printed conditions and prescribed documents differ between states. A general tool has to guess.Built for NSW contracts from the start.
Does it change the review for different buyers?A clause that suits an investor can be a serious problem for a family moving in.Yes. Purchaser lens packs for owner-occupiers, first home buyers, investors, trusts, SMSFs and developers, plus a balanced default when the buyer's plans are not yet known.
Does it cover commercial and off-the-plan contracts?Many firms review a mix of contract types. Using a different process for each costs time.Yes. Established, strata, off-the-plan, unregistered land, house and land and commercial.

The 4 things we built Convex around

Convex is built around purchaser lens packs. The review and letter are framed for an owner-occupier, first home buyer, downsizer, investor, trust, SMSF or developer, so clients are not buried in risks that do not apply to them and the ones that do are not missed.

Every contract type you handle

Residential, strata, off-the-plan, unregistered land, house and land and commercial contracts are all covered in the one subscription, so the whole team uses the same process.

Built from practice

From issue to amendment Coming soon
