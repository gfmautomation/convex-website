Commercial and industrial

AI review of NSW commercial property contracts

For offices, shops, industrial units, warehouses, mixed-use buildings and development sites, whether sold vacant or tenanted. Convex reviews the contract, the vendor's annexures and any leases attached.

What Convex checks

What Convex looks for in commercial contracts

GST

Whether the price is GST inclusive or exclusive, going concern and margin scheme conditions, and what happens if a going concern supply is not achieved.

Leases

Tenancy schedules and leases attached: rent, term, options, reviews, security and bank guarantees, and assignment of the landlord's interest.

Permitted use

Zoning and permitted use from the s10.7 certificate, compared with the buyer's intended use or the tenant's use.

Special conditions

As-is clauses, limits on the purchaser's claims, vendor warranties, and conditions typical of commercial sales.

Adjustments

Outgoings, land tax and rent adjustments, arrears and incentives owed to tenants at completion.

Building and site

Fire safety, environmental and contamination matters, and development consents disclosed in the pack.

Documents

What Convex reads

Convex reviews the contract together with the documents attached to it.

  • The contract and special conditions
  • Title search, plans and 88B instruments
  • Section 10.7 planning certificate
  • Leases, tenancy schedules and disclosure statements attached to the contract
  • Strata plan and by-laws for commercial strata lots
  • Development consents and fire safety documents where included

Questions

Commercial contract review FAQs

Is there a cooling-off period for a commercial contract?
Cooling-off applies to residential property in NSW. Convex confirms the position for the contract and notes any condition that affects it.
Does Convex review standalone commercial leases?
No. Convex reads leases attached to a contract of sale as part of that review. It does not review a lease on its own.
Does Convex review business sale contracts?
No. Convex reviews contracts for the sale of land. Contracts for the sale of a business are outside its scope.
Does Convex give GST advice?
Convex explains how the contract deals with GST and flags inconsistencies, such as a going concern clause with no lease attached. Tax advice remains with the client's accountant.
