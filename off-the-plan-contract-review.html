Off-the-plan strata

AI review of NSW off-the-plan contracts

Off-the-plan contracts carry risks an established sale does not: the building does not exist yet, the plan can change, and completion depends on registration. Convex reviews the contract with its disclosure statement and draft documents.

What Convex checks

What Convex looks for in off-the-plan contracts

Disclosure statement

Whether a disclosure statement and the required draft documents are attached, and whether they are consistent with the contract.

Sunset date

The sunset date, how it can be extended, and the vendor's rights to rescind, compared with the limits that apply to residential off-the-plan contracts.

Changes to the plan

Clauses allowing the vendor to vary the plan, the lot, the finishes or the by-laws, and the purchaser's rights when a material change is notified.

Deposit

Deposit amount, instalments, deposit bonds and guarantees, and where and how the deposit is held until completion.

Completion

How completion is triggered by registration of the plan, the notice period, and any delay, interest or holding costs.

Developer protections

Restrictions on caveats, on-selling, assignment and marketing, nomination fees and clauses limiting claims for defects.

Documents

What Convex reads

Convex reviews the contract together with the documents attached to it.

  • The contract and special conditions
  • Disclosure statement
  • Draft strata plan and plan of the proposed lot
  • Draft by-laws and any proposed strata development contract
  • Schedule of finishes and inclusions
  • Title search for the parent lot
  • Section 10.7 planning certificate and development consent where attached

Questions

Off-the-plan contract review FAQs

What is the cooling-off period for an off-the-plan contract?
Residential off-the-plan contracts in NSW have a longer cooling-off period than an established sale. Convex confirms whether a cooling-off period applies and whether a waiver is proposed.
Does Convex compare the disclosure statement with the contract?
Yes. Convex reads both and flags inconsistencies, for example a different number of car spaces or a different sunset date.
Does Convex review off-the-plan houses or land lots?
Proposed Torrens lots are covered on the unregistered land page. House and land packages are covered on the house and land page.
Does Convex check the finishes schedule against the marketing material?
Convex reads the finishes schedule attached to the contract. Marketing brochures are not part of the contract and are not reviewed.
