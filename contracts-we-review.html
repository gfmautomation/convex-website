What Convex reads in the pack

Convex reviews the contract together with every document attached to it.

DocumentWhat it covers
Contract front pageParties, price, deposit, completion period, inclusions, cooling-off and contract edition
Special conditionsEvery special condition, rated for the buyer
Title search and plansRegistered proprietor, mortgages, caveats, easements and covenants
Section 88B instrumentRestrictions on use, positive covenants and building controls
Section 10.7 planning certificateZoning, permitted use, heritage, flood, bushfire, contamination and acquisition
Sewer service diagramWhether it is attached and relevant to the lot
Swimming pool certificatesCertificate of compliance or non-compliance and what follows for the buyer
Occupation certificates and approvalsCertificates included in the pack and their relevance to the improvements
Home building insuranceInsurance or owner-builder documents where they apply
Strata plan and by-lawsLot, unit entitlement, car spaces, exclusive use and by-law obligations
Off-the-plan disclosure statementConsistency with the contract, draft plan, draft by-laws and finishes
Tenancy agreements and leasesRent, term, options, bond or security and assignment
Out of scope

What Convex does not review

  • Building contracts, including the build side of a house and land package
  • Contracts for the sale of a business
  • Standalone leases not attached to a contract of sale
  • Strata inspection reports and building and pest reports
  • Contracts for property outside NSW
