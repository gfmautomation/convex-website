Strata

AI review of NSW strata contracts

For units, townhouses and villas in a registered strata scheme. Convex reviews the contract together with the strata plan, by-laws and strata disclosures, and flags what matters for the particular buyer.

What Convex checks

What Convex looks for in strata contracts

Strata plan and the lot

Lot number, unit entitlement, and whether car spaces and storage are part of the lot, exclusive use common property, or only a licence.

By-laws

Pets, renovations, short-term letting, parking, smoking and exclusive use by-laws, with any unusual obligations on the owner.

Levies and special levies

How levies and special levies are adjusted, and special conditions that shift levies raised before completion to the purchaser.

Defects and remediation

Acknowledgements about building defects, cladding, rectification orders or remediation works, and clauses limiting the purchaser's claims.

Strata disclosures

Strata renewal proposals, strata development contracts and other disclosures that must accompany a strata contract.

Special conditions

Every special condition reviewed, as for any residential contract.

Documents

What Convex reads

Convex reviews the contract together with the documents attached to it.

  • The contract and special conditions
  • Strata plan and title search for the lot
  • By-laws, including exclusive use and special privilege by-laws
  • Section 10.7 planning certificate
  • Strata renewal and strata development contract disclosures where attached
  • Residential tenancy agreement where tenanted

Questions

Strata contract review FAQs

Does Convex review strata inspection reports?
No. Convex reviews the contract and the strata documents attached to it. A strata inspection report from the owners corporation's records is a separate document and remains recommended.
Can Convex tell whether a car space is on title?
Convex reads the strata plan and by-laws in the pack and reports whether the car space appears to be part of the lot, exclusive use common property, or neither.
Is off-the-plan strata covered here?
Off-the-plan strata has its own checks, including the disclosure statement and sunset date. See off-the-plan contract review.
Does the lens change a strata review?
Yes. A strata review is framed differently for a downsizer moving in than for an investor.
