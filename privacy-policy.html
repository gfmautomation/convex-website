Privacy Policy

Convex Legal Pty Ltd (ABN 87 692 237 577).

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Convex Legal Pty Ltd (ABN 87 692 237 577) ("Convex", "we", "our" or "us") collects, uses, discloses and protects personal information in connection with the Convex contract review platform at convex.au and app.convex.au (the "Service"). We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers 2 groups of people:

  • Our users and prospective users: anyone who uses the Service, signs up for a trial, contacts us or receives our emails, such as lawyers, conveyancers and their staff.
  • People named in documents our users upload: for example purchasers, vendors, registered proprietors and other parties named in a contract for the sale of land, title search or other attached document ("Client Information").

For Client Information, our users are responsible for collecting it from their clients and telling them how it will be handled. We handle it on our users' behalf, only to provide the Service to them.

2. What we collect

  • Account and contact details: name, firm name, email address, phone number, role, practising certificate or licence details where provided, and login and authentication information.
  • Billing information: plan, billing history and billing contact details. Card and payment details are collected and stored by our payment provider, Stripe. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Documents and matter details: contracts, title searches, planning certificates, plans and other documents uploaded for review, matter numbers, client names and the purchaser type selected, and the reports and letters generated. These may contain Client Information such as names, addresses, property details, prices and details of companies, trusts or self-managed super funds.
  • Usage and technical information: log data, device and browser information, IP address, and how the Service and our website are used, collected through cookies and similar tools.
  • Communications: emails, support requests, call notes and feedback.

We do not ask for sensitive information (such as health information). If it appears in an uploaded document, it is handled only as part of that document.

3. How we collect it

We collect personal information directly from you when you sign up, use the Service, buy a subscription, contact us or attend a demonstration; from documents and details our users upload; automatically through cookies and analytics when you use our website or the Service; and from publicly available professional sources, such as firm websites and professional directories, when we contact firms about the Service.

4. Why we collect, use and hold it

  • To provide the Service, including generating contract reviews, reports and letters
  • To create and manage accounts, process payments and provide support
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent misuse and fix faults
  • To improve the Service
  • To communicate with you about your account, changes to the Service and these terms
  • To send you information about Convex, where permitted by law (see section 10)
  • To comply with our legal obligations

We use Client Information only to provide the Service to the user who uploaded it. We do not use Client Information to market to the individuals named in it, and we do not sell personal information.

5. Who we disclose it to

We disclose personal information only as needed for the purposes above, to:

  • Hosting platforms: cloud hosting, database and website hosting providers, which store and run the Service
  • AI processing platforms: document processing and AI service providers, which convert uploaded documents to text and generate reviews (see sections 6 and 7)
  • Payments platforms: Stripe, which processes subscription and credit payments
  • Email and communications platforms: email and communications providers, which we use to send emails and manage correspondence
  • Analytics platforms: website analytics providers, which help us understand how our website is used
  • Integrations you choose to connect: practice management software such as Smokeball or Clio, when you instruct the Service to send or receive matter information
  • Professional advisers, insurers, and regulators or law enforcement: where required or authorised by law

Our service providers are bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations and may use the information only to provide their services to us.

6. Overseas disclosure

Uploaded contract documents and account data are stored in Australia. To convert documents to text and generate reviews, the text of uploaded documents is processed by service providers in the United States. These providers do not use documents submitted through the Service to train their AI models. They hold the content only for as long as needed to provide their service, except that it may be retained for up to 30 days to monitor for misuse before being deleted. Our payment, email, analytics and website hosting providers may also process personal information in the United States and other countries. Where personal information is disclosed overseas, we take reasonable steps to ensure the recipient handles it consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles, including through contractual terms.

7. How the Service uses artificial intelligence

The Service uses an AI language model to read uploaded documents, identify issues, rate them green, amber or red, and draft a letter. The output is a draft for a qualified lawyer or conveyancer to review, edit and approve before it is used.

8. Automated processing and decisions

The Service uses automated processing of personal information in uploaded documents to produce draft reports and letters. The Service does not itself make decisions that significantly affect the rights or interests of any individual: every output is reviewed and approved by the professional user, who remains responsible for the advice given. If you have questions about how automated processing is used, contact us using the details in section 13.

9. Storage, security and retention

We protect personal information with measures that include encryption in transit and at rest, access controls and multi-factor authentication, limited and logged staff access, and regular review of our security practices.

Uploaded documents and outputs are kept in the user's account until the user deletes them or the account is closed, and are then deleted within 90 days, other than limited backups and records we must keep by law. Account and billing records are kept for as long as needed for our business and legal obligations, including tax record-keeping.

If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme. Where the breach affects Client Information, we will also notify the user who uploaded it promptly.

10. Marketing

We may send you information about Convex where you have consented, where it can be inferred from your professional role and our existing relationship, or where your business email address is published and the information is relevant to your work. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also opt out by emailing info@convex.au.

11. Cookies

Our website and the Service use cookies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and understand how the site is used. You can block cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the Service may not work without them.

12. Access, correction and complaints

You may ask for access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you by contacting us. We will respond within a reasonable time, usually 30 days. If your request relates to Client Information uploaded by a lawyer or conveyancer, we may refer you to them.

If you have a complaint about how we have handled your personal information, contact us first and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at www.oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

14. Changes to this policy
