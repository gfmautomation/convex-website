House and land packages

AI review of the land contract in a house and land package

In a house and land package the buyer usually signs 2 contracts: one for the land and a separate building contract. Convex reviews the land contract and flags where it depends on the build. The building contract is outside its scope.

What Convex checks

What Convex looks for in house and land contracts

Links to the builder

Conditions requiring a nominated builder, making the land contract conditional on a building contract, or allowing the vendor to rescind if the build does not proceed.

Timing

Land completion dates against the likely start of construction, and any holding costs or penalties if the build is delayed.

Design approvals

Developer approval of plans, design guidelines and building covenants that the builder must also satisfy.

Registered or unregistered

Whether the land is already registered, and if not, the draft plan, 88B instrument and sunset date.

Deposit and price

The land deposit and any package pricing or rebates referred to in the land contract.

Special conditions

Every special condition in the land contract reviewed.

Documents

What Convex reads

Convex reviews the contract together with the documents attached to it.

  • The land contract and special conditions
  • Title search, or the draft deposited plan for an unregistered lot
  • Section 88B instrument or draft instrument
  • Design guidelines where attached
  • Section 10.7 planning certificate
  • Not reviewed: the building contract, plans and specifications

Questions

House and land contract review FAQs

Why doesn't Convex review the building contract?
Building contracts are a different kind of document with their own statutory framework and industry forms. Convex focuses on the land contract so that part of the package is reviewed in depth.
Should I upload the building contract with the land contract?
Upload the land contract and its annexures only. If the land contract refers to the building contract, Convex notes the reference so you can review the 2 together.
Does Convex cover house and land packages on registered land?
Yes. The land can be registered or unregistered.
Is there a separate fee for house and land reviews?
No. A house and land review uses 1 credit, the same as any other review.
