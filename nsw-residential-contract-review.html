Established residential

AI review of NSW residential contracts for sale

For established houses, townhouses, duplexes and villas on Torrens title, vacant or tenanted. Convex reviews the Law Society and REINSW contract, the vendor's special conditions and every document attached.

What Convex checks

What Convex looks for in established residential contracts

Special conditions

Early deposit release, reduced deposits and deposit bonds, notice to complete periods, penalty interest, vendor rescission rights and clauses excluding the purchaser's claims.

Front page and particulars

Parties, price, deposit, completion period, inclusions and exclusions, whether cooling-off applies or a 66W certificate is proposed, and which edition of the contract is used.

Title and plans

Whether the vendor is the registered proprietor, and the mortgages, caveats, easements, covenants and 88B restrictions recorded on title.

Planning certificate

Zoning and permitted use, heritage, flood, bushfire, contamination, road widening and acquisition affectations from the s10.7 certificate.

Prescribed documents

Whether the documents required for the contract appear to be attached. A missing prescribed document can give the purchaser a right to rescind.

Tenancies

Residential tenancy agreements: parties, rent, term, bond, and whether the property is sold with vacant possession or subject to the tenancy.

Documents

What Convex reads

Convex reviews the contract together with the documents attached to it.

  • The contract: front page, printed conditions and special conditions
  • Title search and the title plan
  • Section 88B instrument and dealings creating easements or covenants
  • Section 10.7 planning certificate
  • Sewer service diagram
  • Swimming pool certificate of compliance or non-compliance
  • Occupation certificates and building approvals included in the pack
  • Home building insurance or owner-builder documents where included
  • Residential tenancy agreement where the property is tenanted

Questions

Established residential contract review FAQs

Does Convex tell me which prescribed documents are missing?
Convex checks the pack against the documents that should be attached for that type of property and flags any that appear to be missing, with the consequence for the purchaser.
Which edition of the contract does Convex support?
Convex reads any edition of the Law Society and REINSW contract for the sale and purchase of land, and flags where an earlier edition appears to have been used.
Can Convex review a tenanted property?
Yes. Convex reads the residential tenancy agreement and explains the tenancy's effect for the buyer.
Does Convex review building and pest reports?
No. Convex reviews the contract and the documents attached to it. Building and pest reports are outside its scope.
How long does a residential review take?
Usually 3 to 5 minutes from upload.
